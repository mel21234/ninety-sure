How sure are you, really?

Most people say “90% sure” and are right about half the time. This is a daily game for the skill behind good guesses: prices, travel times, deadlines, the numbers in the news.

  1. Drag the orange calipers around the numbers you're 90% sure the answer is between.
  2. A tight range earns up to 100 points. Miss the answer and you get nothing.
  3. Truly 90%-sure ranges catch about 9 answers in 10. See how close you get.
