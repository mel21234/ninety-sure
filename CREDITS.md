## Distances between cities
Calculated from city-centre coordinates with the haversine (great-circle) formula, using a mean Earth radius of 6,371 km and rounded to the nearest 10 km.

## Arithmetic, powers and unit conversions
Calculated exactly by the build script, or taken from standard definitions (for example, one inch is exactly 2.54 cm).

## Other facts
Widely documented values for landmarks, geography, space, history and the human body. Where sources disagree, the note shown after the answer says so.
